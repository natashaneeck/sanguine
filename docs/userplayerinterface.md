Design a user-player interface, such that human or AI players could interact with the model, 
and explain your design.

Players, whether human or AI, will need to be able to do 2 core actions, playing cards and 
passing their turn.

When they play cards, they will need to be able to pick from the cards currently in their hand and
specify which one they are picking, and specify where on the board they are placing it.
If they pick an invalid option, the move should not work. 

Passing should always be an option, and will result in an additional card in their hand plus 
the turn passing to the other player.
